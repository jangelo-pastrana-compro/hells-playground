THE STORY

You were never meant to wake here. Nine circles of fire, ice and lies lie between you and the light.

Every floor is a promise. Every promise is a lie.

Survive the traps of Hell. Reach Heaven's Gate.

INSTRUCTIONS

MOVE: Arrow keys / A D   JUMP: Up / W / Space
PAUSE: Esc / P   (touch: on-screen buttons)

Reach the door to finish a level.
Nothing is safe: floors vanish, spikes hide, doors run.
Die and you restart the current level instantly.
5 levels per circle, 9 circles, then Heaven's Gate.
